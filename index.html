      How an engagement runs

      The same four steps apply whether you hire us for advice, a build or a course. You decide at the end of each step whether to continue.

      1. Assess

        We map your processes, data and constraints, and agree what success looks like in numbers.

      2. Prototype

        A working version on your real data, tested with the people who will use it.

      3. Integrate

        We connect it to your systems with security, approvals and monitoring in place.

      4. Hand over

        Your team is trained to operate it, with documentation and a support plan.

      Questions buyers ask first

      How do you work with clients in other time zones?

      The clocks above show the overlap with Dhaka for each city. We agree fixed meeting windows at the start, and between meetings the team works from written updates and a shared board.

      Can training be delivered in English?

      Yes. Programmes run in English or Bengali, on-site or online, using your own documents and tools for the exercises.

      Where does our data go?

      We work under NDA, and we can design workflows that keep your data inside your own cloud or network. We agree the data rules with your security team before any build starts.

      Do you only build AI?

      No. AI work sits on our software engineering practice, so we can also build the web, mobile and integration layers that a working AI system needs.
